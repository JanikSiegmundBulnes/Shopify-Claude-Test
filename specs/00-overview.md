# Bazaar – overview

Goal: one storefront and one cart for products from several public Shopify stores.
Users: shoppers who follow several small brands.
Main features: load catalogs, search and filter across stores, product detail, one cart grouped by store, checkout per store via cart permalinks, manage the list of stores.
Out of scope: payments inside Bazaar, a backend database.
Planned features: F-01 savings in cart, F-02 sort by savings.
